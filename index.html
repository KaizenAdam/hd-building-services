Our Services

From large-scale construction projects to smaller repairs — HD Building Services handles it all with the same commitment to quality.

Extensions & New Builds

Single and double-storey extensions, garage conversions, and new build projects managed from design through to completion.

Renovations & Refurbishments

Full property refurbishments, room remodels, and structural alterations — transforming spaces while keeping disruption to a minimum.

Bricklaying & Blockwork

Skilled bricklaying for walls, foundations, driveways, garden structures, and new builds. Quality pointing and repointing work too.

Carpentry & Internal Work
